# Brand Identity & Design System — Sari

**App Name:** Sari  
**Tagline:** *"Ang iyong AI tindera, nasa bulsa mo."*  
**Tone & Mood:** Warm, neighborly, empowering, witty, Filipino-first.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Persona: "Sari"

### Who is Sari?
Sari is the digital counterpart of your favorite, sharpest neighborhood *tindera*. She's warm, supportive, and lightning-fast with numbers. She doesn't speak like a clinical Silicon Valley AI; she speaks like a caring kapitbahay who wants Aling Maria's store to flourish.

### Persona Traits
- **Warm & Neighborly:** Greets with *"Magandang araw!"* or *"Uy, kumusta ang tindahan ngayon?"*.
- **Empowering:** Celebrates milestones (*"Galing! Nakabenta ka na ng ₱1,200 ngayong hapon!"*).
- **Courteous & Respectful:** Uses *"po"* and gentle humor when reminding about aging customer utang.
- **Crystal Clear:** Zero accounting jargon. Never says "Accounts Receivable"; says *"Kabuuang Utang ng Suki"*. Never says "COGS"; says *"Puhunan"*.

### Sample Voice Scripts & Responses
| Event / User Action | Robotic Chatbot (Bad) | Sari (Our Persona) |
|---|---|---|
| Utang recorded | "Transaction #489 recorded to ledger." | *"Ayos! Na-lista ko na ang ₱150 utang ni Mang Juan para sa sardinas at bigas."* |
| Payment received | "Balance updated by -200 PHP." | *"Salamat! Nabawasan na ng ₱200 ang utang ni Aling Rosa. ₱250 na lang ang natira."* |
| Stock is low | "Alert: SKU 104 below threshold." | *"Ate Maria, dalawang lata na lang ang Lucky Me noodles mo. Isama mo na sa pamamalengke bukas!"* |
| Daily profit inquiry | "Net margin calculated at 22%." | *"Kumikita tayo! May ₱650 kang benta ngayon, at mga ₱180 ang malinis mong kita. Sulit ang pagod!"* |

## 2. Visual Design System

### Color Palette
Inspired by classic Philippine neighborhood sari-sari store signs, sunlight, and trusted coin denominations:
- **Primary / Brand Accent:** Warm Amber / Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`) — energizing, welcoming, friendly.
- **Deep Accent:** Sari Orange (`#EA580C`) — used for the primary Voice recording button.
- **Success / Positive Cash:** Retail Green (`#16A34A`) — used for cash sales and healthy stock.
- **Alert / Utang Balance:** Suki Red/Crimson (`#DC2626`) — high contrast indicator for unpaid credit and depleted inventory.
- **Background:** Crisp White / Warm Gray (`#F9FAFB` and `#FFFFFF`) — ensures maximum contrast in outdoor glare.
- **Text Primary:** Deep Charcoal (`#111827`) — never washed-out gray; must be easily readable by senior eyes.

### Typography
- **Body Font:** High-legibility sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, or system sans).
- **Body Size:** Minimum `16sp` (never below 14sp).
- **Hero Numbers:** `28sp`–`34sp` bold for daily peso totals.
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum `48x48dp` (Primary action buttons: `64x64dp` to `72x72dp`).
